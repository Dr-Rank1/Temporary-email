Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Temporary email (“TempBox”, “the app”) is a disposable-inbox client for Android. This policy describes what the app stores, what third parties see, and how to remove data.

What we collect

The app does not require your name, real email address, or phone number. It does not operate its own account system.

What stays on your device

The generated mailbox address, password, and session token are stored only on the device, in encrypted preferences. Theme, refresh interval, and similar settings are stored locally. This data is excluded from Android backup.

Mail hosting

Messages are created and stored by mail.tm for up to 7 days. When you generate a new address or reset the app, TempBox asks mail.tm to delete the previous account. mail.tm’s own policy applies to mail they host.

Advertising

Ads are served by Start.io. The app declares the advertising ID permission because that SDK may use it. Location permissions that the SDK might merge in are removed from the app manifest. We do not sell your personal information.

Notifications and autofill

If you allow notifications, the app can surface detected one-time codes while it is running. If you enable the autofill service, the current disposable address can be offered in email fields in other apps.

Internet access

The app needs the internet to talk to mail.tm and to load ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The date at the top will be revised.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: github.com/Dr-Rank1/Temporary-email/issues
